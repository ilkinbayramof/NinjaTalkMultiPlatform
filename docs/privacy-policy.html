English

This Privacy Policy explains how the Psst mobile application ("Psst", "the app", "we") collects, uses and protects your information. By using Psst you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Psst is an anonymous chat application for adults (18+). For any privacy question, contact us at psstanonymouschat@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and password (passwords are stored in hashed form).
  • Profile information: gender, date of birth, biography (bio) and an optional profile photo.
  • Messages: the content of messages you send and receive in the app, and their timestamps.
  • Safety information: the list of users you have blocked.
  • Purchase information: records of in-app purchases (such as Premium or Boost). Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your card details.
  • Device information: a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver message notifications to your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, or advertising identifier, and we do not show advertising.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your profile to other users and let you discover and chat with them.
  • To deliver your messages and send you notifications about new messages.
  • To provide purchased features such as Premium and Boost.
  • To keep the community safe: enforcing blocks, reviewing reports and removing abusive accounts.

4. What other users can see

Other users can see your profile information (gender, age derived from your date of birth, bio and profile photo) and the messages you send to them. Your email address is never shown to other users.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – to deliver push notifications.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store – to process in-app purchases.
  • Hosting providers – our servers store account data and messages.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Data security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on secured servers. Access is protected with authentication tokens. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account. When you delete your account, your profile and associated data are deleted from our systems. You can also request deletion by email; see the account deletion page.

8. Your rights

You can access and update your profile in the app, delete your account, and turn off notifications in your device settings. You may contact us to request a copy or deletion of your data.

9. Age requirement

Psst is intended only for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will delete the account.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

11. Contact

Email: psstanonymouschat@gmail.com

Profilinizi tətbiqdə görə və dəyişə, hesabınızı silə və bildirişləri cihaz tənzimləmələrindən söndürə bilərsiniz. Məlumatlarınızın surətini və ya silinməsini tələb etmək üçün bizimlə əlaqə saxlaya bilərsiniz.

Psst yalnız 18 yaş və yuxarı istifadəçilər üçündür. 18 yaşından kiçik şəxslərdən bilərəkdən məlumat toplamırıq. İstifadəçinin 18 yaşından kiçik olduğu məlum olarsa, hesab silinəcək.
